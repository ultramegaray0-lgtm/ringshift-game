HOW TO PLAY

Rotate the rings. Every beam fires straight through the center. Light every LOCK at once to breach.

// CONTROLS

▲▼ select ring ◀▶ rotate selected ring Drag a ring's track directly Esc pause Gamepad D-pad / stick + A
Tip: click any ring's track to select it, then drag left/right to rotate — or use the arrow keys.

// FIELD LEGEND

◉
SourceFires a beam straight through the center of the ring it's on.
╲
MirrorTurns another ring's beam exactly 90°, at any slot. ╲ turns it clockwise, ╱ turns it counter-clockwise.
⬤
BlockDestroys another ring's beam outright. Never its own.
▲
AmplifierBoosts another ring's beam to STRONG, letting it punch through other rings' sources.
◎
Portal (Black Hole)Holes come in a linked pair. Each hole is TWO nodes long: a beam touching it anywhere along its whole length, from ANY direction, is absorbed. It then comes out of the OTHER hole, always travelling in that hole’s own fixed exit direction (where its black mouth points) — never the direction the beam came in on. Two holes in a level can point different ways.
◐
Half-BlockOne-way valve — stops another ring's beam from ONE direction (in or out) only.
△
PrismSplits another ring's beam into two, bent ±45° off its original heading.
◍
Echo (Satellite)Always pinned in place. Its DISH shows which way it fires, and it is built pointing UP, DOWN, LEFT or RIGHT depending on the level. A beam that arrives from the DISH side (within 45° of the dish axis) dies there. A beam that arrives from either SIDE or from the ENGINE end (below) is absorbed and relaunched straight out of the DISH.
◎
LockNeeds a beam landing on it. ALL Locks must be lit at once to breach the level.
☆
Bonus LockOptional — light it too, at the same time as every Lock, for a bonus coin. Never required.

// ☄ SPECIAL MODE — METEOR ATTACK

☄
Meteor Lock DestructionEvery Special Mode level has 6 coin Locks. Every 10 seconds a meteor picks one at complete random and destroys it — gone for good, whether it was lit or dark. Being lit does NOT protect a Lock. Nothing about rings, beams, or gimmicks changes; the clock only takes away targets. The run always ends in a win — the instant every surviving Lock is lit at once, it's over. The goal is having as many lit as possible when that happens.

// ✦ SPACE ORCHESTRA — FOG, THE GRAY UFO & RING COINS

🌫️
FogEvery Lock in Space Orchestra is fogged. While the fog is active it's a physical wall — a beam that reaches a fogged Lock is blocked there, no matter how correctly the rings are set, and the Lock's true state stays hidden.
🛸
Gray UFOEvery movement hides one gray UFO behind Blocks and Mirrors. Route a beam onto it and the fog lifts for a few seconds, then comes back one Lock at a time, 5 seconds apart. If you have a Lock lit, the fog holds off until you move a ring. The UFO flees 30 seconds after the movement begins — if it escapes you may not be able to win; reset and try again.
🪙
Ring CoinsEvery ring that fires a beam carries a gold Coin. Rotating that ring spends its Coin's charge; after 9 rotations of that ring, its Coin is destroyed for good. Rings never stop rotating and losing a Coin never fails the movement — but a Coin still sitting safely on the board looks better than an empty ring. RESET always restores every ring's Coin, rotation count, the fog, and the Bell.
Find this again anytime from Options.
